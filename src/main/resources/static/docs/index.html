Timetable Scheduler

Upload your school's departments, lecturers, rooms, courses and students. Get back a clash-free weekly timetable.

What it does

The scheduler finds a time and a room for every class in your school's week, so that:

  • no lecturer is in two places at once,
  • no student has two classes at the same time,
  • no room is booked twice, and every class gets a room big enough for it,
  • nothing is placed in your daily break or outside your school day.

The result is available as a PDF and can also be sent automatically to your own system.

How it works, in five steps

  1. Register your school and set your school day and break times. Getting started
  2. Upload your data as CSV files or JSON: departments, lecturers, rooms, programmes, courses, curriculum and students. Uploading your data
  3. Create an academic period, such as 2026/2027 First Semester.
  4. Upload the period's sections and enrollments: which classes run this period, who teaches them, and which students take them.
  5. Generate. The scheduler works in the background and tells you when the timetable is ready. Generating a timetable

What you'll need

  • Your school's data, in either form:
    • a spreadsheet you can export to CSV (Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers all work), or
    • a system that can send JSON, such as your student records or HR system, to keep the data in sync automatically.
  • A tool for sending web requests. These docs use curl, which is built into macOS, Linux and Windows 10+. You can also use the API reference and send requests from your browser, without installing anything.

Want to try it first? Download the sample CSV files, a complete example school, and follow the guides with them.
